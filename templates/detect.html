Gesture Recognition System for Restaurant Communication

This web application helps users communicate in restaurants using hand gestures. The system detects gesture words, forms a sentence, and converts the sentence into voice output. Immerse in the golden experience — accessible & elegant.

Starting instruction screen in: 10 seconds
HOW TO USE

Command Gesture Instructions

Use these three control gestures to manage your sentence & voice output

🖐️ start_command

Use this gesture to begin a new sentence. When this gesture is given, the old sentence is cleared and prediction starts.

Start New Sentence

✋ stop_command

Use this gesture after completing your sentence. This stops normal word prediction and waits for the speak command.

Stop Prediction

🗣️ speak_command

Use this gesture to read the formed sentence as voice. The predicted sentence will be spoken aloud.

Speak Sentence
